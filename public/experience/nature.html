The bigger picture

Where does it go?

Litter does not disappear when it leaves the path. It can reach streams, harm wildlife and damage the places people came to enjoy.

Wildlife harmed

Animals may mistake litter for food or become caught in it.

Polluted streams

Rain carries waste from trails and campsites into nearby water.

Fire risk

Carelessly discarded glass and smoking materials can increase hazards in dry areas.

Lost beauty

Litter changes the experience for every visitor who comes after you.

One last thing
