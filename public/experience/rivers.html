01 — The first flush

It starts on the street.

A shower of rain hits the city. Water runs along the kerb, picking up whatever has been left behind.

A wrapper on the pavement may be only a few metres from a storm drain.

The route can change

A cleaner river begins before the riverbank.

Good waste systems matter. So do everyday choices and reporting places where waste is being dumped or drains are overflowing.
