The bigger picture

What reaches the river reaches us.

A river is connected to the lives around it. Pollution can affect people, wildlife and the places that depend on its water.

The river carries more than water. What is thrown away on its banks can travel far beyond the place it was dropped.
The consequences
Drinking water

Pollution makes clean water harder and more costly to provide. Water needs proper treatment before it is safe to drink.

Fish and wildlife

Plastic and polluted water can harm fish, birds and other animals that depend on rivers.

Farms and food

River water is used in agriculture. Poor water quality can affect soil, crops and the wider food chain.

Health

Untreated or contaminated water can spread disease when people come into contact with it or use it without adequate treatment.
