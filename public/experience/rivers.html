The bigger picture

Where does the water go?

The river connects homes, farms, wildlife and communities. Pollution at one stop can affect people and ecosystems far downstream.

Drinking water

Pollution makes clean water harder and more expensive to provide.

Fish and wildlife

Plastic and polluted water can harm aquatic animals and birds.

Farms and food

Water quality affects irrigation, soil and the food chain.

Disease

Contaminated water can spread illness when it is not treated safely.
